# Group video outline (Part 2, 35%)

**Rules from the brief:** 5–6 minutes. Use 3–5 key artifacts to show Sense, Frame, Investigate, Create and Refine. **At least two members present with their faces visible.** The group leader uploads to Mediasite and submits the link on Learning Mall by **13 Dec, 23:59 Beijing time**. Then **open the submitted link yourselves** to check that it plays (a broken link counts as no submission).

The video is about **how the project developed**, not a product advert. The four marking criteria have equal weight (25% each): Context & Problem, Investigation & Evidence, Evolution & Iteration, Collaboration & Reflection.

This outline is a starting point. **Write the spoken words yourselves.** Each speaker should talk about work they actually did.

## Suggested artifacts (pick 3–5)

1. **Artifact 2: Project Brief v5.** Problem statement, HMW question, stakeholder map.
2. **Artifact 3: Evidence Matrix.** TrojanSense + Rawat et al., and the survey (51 recorded responses).
3. **Early prototype:** the Tinkercad LDR circuit (simulation: 54 → 974) and/or the first dashboard screenshot from 4 Nov.

5. **Final demo:** a short clip of the dashboard + phone form + alert + review.
